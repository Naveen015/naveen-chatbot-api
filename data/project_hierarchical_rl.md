# Project: Trajectory-Aware Human Feedback for Hierarchical RL

**Role**: Deep RL Engineer  
**Duration**: Sep 2024 – Dec 2024  

---

## Overview
A Hierarchical Reinforcement Learning (HRL) framework incorporating human feedback and subgoal generation for complex control tasks.

## Key Technical Achievements (STAR Format)
* **Situation**: Complex, long-horizon reinforcement learning environments suffer from sparse rewards and inefficient exploration.
* **Task**: Develop a trajectory-aware human feedback mechanism for hierarchical policy optimization.
* **Action**: Formulated a multi-level HRL architecture where high-level policies generate subgoals based on trajectory-aware human feedback signals, while low-level policies optimize motor control. Executed training over 500,000+ simulation steps.
* **Result**: Evaluated in OpenAI Gym FetchReach, achieving a 10% increase in task completion success rate and an 18% reduction in unnecessary environment exploration.
