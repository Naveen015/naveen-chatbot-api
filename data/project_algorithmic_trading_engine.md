# Project: Algorithmic Trading Engine

**Role**: Quant Developer  
**Duration**: Jun 2024 – Aug 2024  

---

## Overview
A high-frequency vectorized backtesting engine and mean-reversion trading model framework.

## Key Technical Achievements (STAR Format)
* **Situation**: Evaluating high-frequency trading strategies across decade-long datasets requires high-speed vectorized computation and realistic transaction cost modeling.
* **Task**: Build a Pandas-vectorized backtesting engine processing 25M+ rows of OHLC market data.
* **Action**: Designed a high-performance backtesting engine modeling execution slippage, bid-ask spreads, and exchange fees across 10 years of market data. Implemented RSI mean-reversion models with walk-forward cross-validation and grid-search parameter optimization.
* **Result**: Achieved an out-of-sample Sharpe ratio of ~0.9 with a 33% reduction in maximum drawdown compared to buy-and-hold benchmarks.
